## The short version

Tether does not ask for your name, your email, or any account. It does not
collect anything you type. The only thing it stores is your high score, and that
stays on your phone.

The app does show ads, and the advertising network that serves them collects
some information. That is described below.

## What the game itself stores

- Your best score, best combo, and number of runs.

This is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted
anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Advertising

Tether shows ads supplied by **Google AdMob**. To serve them, Google may collect
and process:

- your device's **advertising ID**
- general device information such as model, operating system version and language
- coarse, IP-derived location
- your interactions with the ads themselves

Google acts as an independent controller of that data. What it collects and how
it is used is described in:

- [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### Your choices

- **Reset or delete your advertising ID** - Android: Settings -> Privacy -> Ads.
- **Personalised ads** - if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app
  asks for your consent the first time it runs, and you can change that answer
  later from the privacy options in the same dialog.

## Children

Tether is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any
information from them.

## Permissions

- **Internet** - to fetch ads. The game itself works offline.
- **Vibrate** - haptic feedback during play.
